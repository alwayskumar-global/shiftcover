Why a page, not a drawer

DecisionSingle full page. The task is a short sequence (describe, check, approve money), and the manager needs the form and the cost visible together while they edit the rate or premium.
Not a drawerA right drawer would cover the roster, which has nothing to do with this task, and would squeeze the PayPal slot and the money table into about 440px while also holding the form. The page also gives a stable URL to return to after PayPal.
MoneyRecomputed on every keystroke in base rate and premium. Worker pay at the cap = hours × rate × (1 + premium). Fee = 5% of that (variable platform_fee_pct). Hold = pay + fee. Editing anything after confirming returns to "needs confirmation".
PayPalThe dashed rectangle is a placeholder for the SDK button (52px high, full column width, max 420px). Click it to simulate the outcome chosen in the bar. In production the SDK owns the button; remove the dashed outline.
Reduced motionShimmer, pulse and spinners stop; "AI is reading this", "Reading…" and "Waiting for PayPal…" are text, so state is still clear.
